- Plan and audit include only safe binding/node/entry/closure identity digests and `launch_mode=bound`.
- Historical Stage 62 audits remain unbound facts and cannot be retroactively upgraded.

- Rollback is simply not selecting bound mode; deleting/rotating a real binding needs separate authorization.
